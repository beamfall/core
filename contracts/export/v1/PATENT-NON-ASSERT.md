# Patent non-assert

Beamfall and each contributor to this specification promise not to assert any
patent claim they control that is necessarily infringed solely by implementing
the normative Beamfall Export Format specification, against any person or entity
for making, using, selling, offering for sale, importing, or distributing a
conforming implementation.

This promise is irrevocable except against a person or entity that first asserts
a patent claim against Beamfall, a Beamfall contributor, or a conforming
implementation based on implementation of this specification. It does not grant
rights to trademarks, trade dress, or claims infringed by technology beyond what
the normative specification necessarily requires.
